# Illustration prompts

Four original portrait illustrations generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then resized and encoded as WebP for mobile delivery. The final assets are `assets/atelier.webp`, `assets/dungeon.webp`, `assets/mirror.webp`, and `assets/triumph.webp`. The cover and chapters share these assets; the full-art viewer preserves the portrait composition.

## atelier

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: richly painted fantasy storybook illustration for a mobile interactive story, portrait 2:3 composition. Museum-quality oil-and-gouache fantasy art with visible brushwork, deep ink-blue shadows, warm antique-gold light, magical cyan accents, atmospheric depth, exquisitely detailed but clear focal subject. A vaguely French game-maker named Monsieur Reiveaux, early thirties, warm olive skin, hazel eyes, distinctive long wavy dark chestnut hair tied loosely, burgundy velvet longcoat with ornate gold lapels, cream poet shirt, gold sunburst brooch, ink-stained fingers. NO text, lettering, logos, watermark, frame, montage, panels, or UI. Original fantasy world; no existing franchise characters. Scene: male Reiveaux works intently at a wooden desk high in a French-inspired gothic atelier at night. He is implementing a game: one hand drawing a dungeon map in an open notebook, the other fitting miniature dungeon rooms together beside a softly glowing enchanted laptop with abstract unlettered shapes. A small twenty-sided golden die is on the desk. Beyond the arched window is a vast stormy fantasy citadel and the shadow of an immense dragon. Expressive intelligent face, inviting but epic atmosphere. Subject face and working hands clearly visible in the middle, atmospheric architecture above.
